⚠ Systemic timeout

StreamFlix stalls every TV/anime-series request to the full 20s ceiling

StreamFlix's episode lookup opens a WebSocket to a Firebase realtime-DB endpoint with its own 30s internal timeout — but the server's per-provider cap is 20s, so it's cut off first, every time, before ever succeeding or reaching its own failure point. Movies never touch this path and were unaffected (2.9s when tested directly). Recommend either lowering PROVIDER_TIMEOUT_MS so TV requests fail faster, or fixing/removing the WebSocket path — as-is, it adds ~20s of dead weight to every single TV and anime-series lookup.
