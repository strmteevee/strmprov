Usenet Movie/Series Search

Searches free public Usenet indexers (NZBKing, Binsearch) and shows only releases that look like a single, directly playable video file — compressed/split archives (RAR, ZIP, 7z, numbered .NNN volumes) are filtered out. Playback downloads the file via your own configured Usenet provider first, then plays it with the browser's built-in video element — container/codec support depends on your browser (MP4/H.264 is the safest bet; HEVC and some MKV audio tracks may not play natively).
